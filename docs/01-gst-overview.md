# GST on commercial rent — the basics

Renting out **commercial** property is a taxable supply of service under GST.
For every month's rent you must:

1. Raise a **tax invoice** on the tenant.
2. Charge **18% GST** on the rent (the *taxable value*).
3. Report the invoice in **GSTR-1** and pay the tax in **GSTR-3B**.

Because all the properties here are in Tamil Nadu and the tenants are in Tamil
Nadu, the supply is **intra-state**. Intra-state GST splits into two equal halves:

| Component | Rate | On 1,00,000 rent |
|-----------|------|------------------|
| CGST      | 9%   | 9,000            |
| SGST      | 9%   | 9,000            |
| **Total** | 18%  | **18,000**       |

So the tenant pays `rent + 18%`, and that 18% is handed over to the government.

> Residential rent to an unregistered person is usually exempt — these units are
> all **commercial**, so 18% applies.
